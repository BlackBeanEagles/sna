How to read the match

Controls were eligible if they had neither played nor encountered the target opening at the matching time. Later exposure is counted as a crossover, not used to choose controls with knowledge of the future.

Rating and activity are measured before matching. The player-cluster bootstrap reflects repeated players in both pair roles; opponent choice and repertoire remain confounders.

03 / Method

From games to trials

These figures use the available January 2016 game sample. Rechecking the original archive and testing another month remain open.

01

Define exposure

An opponent plays an ECO opening against a player. Opening and colour stay together in the behaviour key.

02

Follow for 14 days

Each included exposure has a fully observable 14-day window. Late positive and negative trials are both excluded.

03

Compare alternatives

Use 20 timestamp shuffles, eligible matched controls, and group-held-out prediction to challenge a simple reading of the raw curve.

04 / Limits

What the result cannot settle

  • Single-month evidence. The analysis has not yet been replicated on a second archive month.
  • Observational exposure. Opponent choice, repertoire, activity and changing opening popularity can shape both exposure and adoption.
  • Repeated observations. One player and one eventual adoption can contribute multiple exposure trials; trial-level intervals overstate precision.
  • Archive recheck pending. The analysis uses previously saved games. Reading the original archive again is a separate validation step.
