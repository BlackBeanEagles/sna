0001 | """Features for the held-out prediction check, built without leakage.
0002 |
0003 | Two problems with the original check, both fixed here.
0004 |
0005 | **Leakage.** The original model computed summary features over the whole
0006 | month. Here fixed background features are computed before the first evaluated
0007 | trial; exposure features are measured at each trial. Whole player-behaviour
0008 | spells are assigned to one side of the held-out split.
0009 |
0010 | **A missing explanation.** The original feature set asked only "who played this
0011 | opening at you?". De Marzo and Servedio [4] showed that a player's next opening
0012 | is largely predicted by how close it sits to what they already play -- an
0013 | "adjacent possible" effect in opening space, with no social component at all.
0014 | Leaving that out makes the social features look better than they are, because
0015 | they are the only candidate on offer. `repertoire_density` puts the competing
0016 | explanation in the model.
0017 |
0018 | Three feature blocks are produced, so the three can be compared directly:
0019 |
0020 |     BASELINE    popularity, rating, activity        "popular openings get played"
0021 |     REPERTOIRE  + density, repertoire size          "you learn what is near what you know"
0022 |     NETWORK     + k, d, sd, degree, kcore, between  "who played it at you"
0023 | """
