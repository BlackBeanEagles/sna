0001 | """Steps 3-4 -- exposure/adoption logs and the trial table.
0002 |
0003 | A behaviour is (opening, colour). One game between white=u and black=v with
0004 | opening X produces two performances and two exposures:
0005 |
0006 |     u performs (X, W)  ->  exposes v to (X, W)
0007 |     v performs (X, B)  ->  exposes u to (X, B)
0008 |
0009 | Adoption of behaviour b by player p = the FIRST time p performs b.
0010 |
0011 | The trial table is the unit of analysis. One row per (player, behaviour,
0012 | k-th exposure) where the player had not yet performed the behaviour:
0013 |
0014 |     k   cumulative exposure events so far
0015 |     d   distinct exposing opponents so far
0016 |     sd  structural diversity: connected components of the subgraph induced
0017 |         on those d exposers, using only edges that exist by time t_k
0018 |         (Ugander et al. 2012)
0019 |     adopted  1 if the player first performs b within ADOPTION_WINDOW_DAYS
0020 |
0021 | Holding k fixed and varying d (or sd) is the decisive comparison.
0022 | """
